# GL-07b: evaluation validity and full-fragment protocol

Session date: 2026-09-17 America/New_York. Runtime model: `gpt-5.6-luna` at the OpenAI Responses API. Development model: Astra High.

## Decision

Retain the production P0 insertion protocol. The experimental P4 full-fragment protocol was faster, but its recognized quality was lower and it produced many more empty insertions. The protocol is safe because it never replaces typed source: a response that does not begin with the exact typed fragment becomes no suggestion.

Machine-readable results are summarized in [the remaining-objectives summary](2026-09-17-remaining-summary.json); captures and reviews are preserved in [the artifact index](2026-09-17-remaining-artifact-index.json).

P4 minus P0 was -13.9 percentage points on the primary family-paired proxy; the descriptive 95% family bootstrap interval was -41.7 to +16.7 points across 12 families. P4 matched 4/9 Go, 0/6 JSON, 0/9 Python, 0/6 TypeScript and 3/6 YAML samples. P0 matched 3/9, 3/6, 3/9, 0/6 and 3/6 respectively.

Seven P4 responses failed the exact typed-prefix check and four merely repeated the already typed fragment; all eleven safely yielded no insertion. Offline parsing found 27/36 P0 and 31/36 P4 processed reconstructions syntactically valid, with no raw-valid response made invalid by processing. Syntax is not behavior.

## Outcome

P4 demonstrates a safe way to derive insertion-only text from a reconstructed fragment, but this prompt did not establish a useful quality tradeoff. It remains benchmark-only. Production keeps P0 and the empty replacement range. The 56-case holdout remains unused for live model inference.

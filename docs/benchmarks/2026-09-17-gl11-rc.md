## Remaining human evidence

The acceptance criterion requiring at least five real coding sessions, primarily Go, is not satisfied by automated tests. Record the stable configuration, session duration, automatic/explicit opportunities, requests/cancellations, accepted characters per active minute, and a short distraction rating. Also exercise physical Tab, partial type-through, another completion provider, credential setup/removal, and restricted workspaces. Do not include source, filenames or repository names in the report.

Luna's observed live request p50s remain roughly 0.75–2.4 seconds across these synthetic studies, with p95s often above 1.5 seconds. That is a material usability risk for automatic ghost text and the main question for dogfood. The release candidate does not claim production-quality tab completion until the human gate is complete.
